# 04 — Play a shared Tic-Tac-Toe match

## Purpose

Let two connected participants play one familiar Tic-Tac-Toe match with matching boards and enforced turn order.

## Acceptance criteria

- Connected participants are assigned X and O roles.
- Both participants see the same board state.
- Only the participant whose turn is active can make a move.
- A valid move is promptly reflected on both boards.
- The game identifies when the match has finished and records its outcome.
- No chat, spectators, rankings, accounts, or matchmaking are required.
