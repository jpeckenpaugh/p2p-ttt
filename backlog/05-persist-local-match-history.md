# 05 — Persist local match history

## Purpose

Keep each participant's own durable local record of a match, including its ordered moves and outcome.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each participant locally records the moves received during a completed match in their own browser workspace.
- Each participant locally records the completed match outcome.
- A participant's completed match history remains available after reloading the application.
- A participant's history does not depend on a shared server, account, or the other participant being online.
